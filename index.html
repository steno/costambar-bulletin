APC Secretary’s Corner

Our perimeter wall needs the community again

Built around 2001 with support from the Valerino Group and APC fundraising — including Lianna Swerdlin’s legendary weekend appeals outside Jenny’s Market — the wall made Costambar far safer. More than 20 years later, age and collapses demand permanent repairs.

  • Temporary fencing installed on the Javillar side (Sep 2026)
  • Security Committee has spent more than RD$500,000 on wall & security improvements
  • Western dirt-trail access near Calle Colón still needs closing
  • Permanent Javillar repairs estimated at several thousand dollars

Donate for security — APC office (Mon–Fri 9–3, Sat 9–12), note “for security”; Jack Cameron at jackflor@aol.com; or costambar.org

In & Around the Community

Sustainable beaches workshop

APC joined the Beach Vendors Association, Junta de Vecinos, Clean Oceans, the Vice Mayor, Ministry of Environment, fishermen, and UASD for a Ministry-hosted workshop on managing sustainable beaches.

A Costambar committee is now forming. Needs on our public beach include eco-friendly sanitation & showers, better waste & seaweed removal, consistent water, sidewalks, parking upgrades, fisheries enforcement, better policing, and weekend lifeguards.

Voluntary gate collections that once funded lifeguards were halted in June 2024 — APC will raise this again with local government.

Conservation

Reef nurseries thriving — and turtle hatchlings on our beach

Led by APC member Deborah Sorenson with the Ministry of Environment and Océanos Limpios. Illegal fishing still threatens juveniles — donations will fund a beachfront watchman.

Contact Deborah on WhatsApp: 849-473-1989. Follow & volunteer via Facebook: Costambar Coral Reef Healing.

A sizeable turtle nest hatched recently. Security and local officials helped hatchlings reach the sea — sometimes up to 90% never make it without protection.

Please read

The beach can be a dangerous place

Costambar experienced another drowning this past week. Historically, most incidents relate to: inability to swim, poor fitness, alcohol, or strong surges between reefs on an outgoing tide.

  • There are currently no regular lifeguards (except some major holidays via Civil Defense)
  • Avoid the water on a strong outgoing tide
  • Never mix alcohol with swimming
  • Report unsafe water activity to POLITUR immediately

Mon–Fri 7:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. · Sat 7:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. · Closed Sundays & holidays.
